# Limitations

- Source CT images (LIDC-IDRI, LNDb, RIDER Lung CT) are not redistributed; reproduction requires downloading them from their original repositories.
- Three folds were aborted by the training-collapse monitor (no learning at epoch 300); only their `checkpoint_latest.pth` exists.
- `checkpoint_best.pth` files are not included (size limit); they are kept by the authors and available on request.
- Validation probability maps (`.npz`) and full test-set predictions are not included (size); they can be regenerated with stage 10 and the provided checkpoints.
- Function docstrings, command-line help texts and some log messages are in Portuguese, as in the executed code.
- Stages 00–10, 26 and 27 have no per-run SHA-256 record of the script file; their versions are those of the frozen pipeline folder.
- Regenerable intermediate products (features, registered masks, preprocessed volumes), logs and audit records are not included; they are regenerated by the pipeline or kept by the authors.
- File names of images and masks keep the identifiers of the source datasets (e.g. `LIDC-IDRI-0001`) so that they remain traceable to the originals.
- The verifier reduces false positives under the examined conditions; no global diagnostic gain or clinical benefit is claimed.
